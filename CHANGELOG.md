# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Every night redrawn as an immersion theme: its ground and all its inks come from one scene.
- Every day redrawn from its night: each role keeps its hue, on a paper tinted by the scene.
- Polaire, a new flavor: a polar night and pale ice under a low sun.
- Vitrail, stained glass, replaces Quartz's high-contrast pair: lumina-vitrail-dark and lumina-vitrail-light.
- Selections, search matches, line numbers and punctuation derived again from the new grounds.

## 1.0.0

- First release: fourteen flavors (Rayleigh, Cherenkov, Quartz, Olive, Dusk, Oxblood, Lagoon, Ember, Canopy, Harbor, Espresso, Aurora, Parchment, Nebula) in dark and light and Quartz's high-contrast pair: 30 themes.
- An independent review applied: amber instead of dark gold by day, deep teal where the night has a cyan; no khaki neutral; selections and lamp-tinted surfaces in the ground's family.
- Indigo gets a palette and an indigo night of its own; Oxblood a night turned toward the wine; Quartz a true neutral.
- Mist, a calm flavor: a hushed night, softer inks, one ink near grey.
- Sienna and Aquarelle archived in archive/: they doubled Lapis.
- A second independent review applied: Rime, a frost flavor on a mid-dark polar night.
- Day warnings a step darker than the code's orange, never brown.
- Quartz takes coral keywords at night and crimson by day; Oxblood rose keywords and honey functions.
- Mist's builtins set apart from its comments; a more visible current line; the day's changed diff lines in light violet.
- Ink and Carmine archived in archive/: they doubled each other.
- A third independent review applied: Lapis in blue and gold at night (calcite builtins); a frank vermilion by day instead of rust.
- Indigo's night turns violet, apart from Lapis; the twin day inks of Quartz and Rime set apart; Oxblood's lamp back to rose.
- Slate, a restrained flavor: keywords in the colour of the text, blue for what is defined, apricot and coral for values.
- Cherenkov, the blue glow of a reactor core under water: an electric azure, uranium-green functions, a near-black steel night.
- Two styles for the colourful flavors: colorful, the default, and focus, one dominant hue on keywords and builtins (-focus).
- Emacs keeps 30 themes: lumina-themes-background and lumina-themes-style choose the variant they load (themes/variants), M-x lumina-themes-reload applies a change; Neovim: :colorscheme lumina.
- Declared variables take the builtin colour in Neovim and VS Code too, as in Emacs; WezTerm and base24 have no focus files.
- In VS Code, `self` and `cls` take the constants ink, as in Neovim; the chosen line of the quick pick takes the selection colour.
- A fourth independent review applied: twin inks set apart (Canopy's rose builtins turn coral, Lapis takes ultramarine keywords), one red-orange ink per day, no olive nor brown, focus builtins never brighter than the keywords.
- Search occurrences take the ground with a little of the cursor's colour, apart from the selection; a neutral selection in Cherenkov, Quartz and Oxblood.
- Slate archived in archive/: at night it was Mist with plain keywords. Ember, a warm graphite night and an ivory paper, takes its place.
- A fifth independent review applied: night search occurrences in a neutral grey instead of khaki or brown; Canopy's day keywords in leaf green, its rust on the strings and the lamp; Cherenkov's night in electric blue, apart from Petrol; Indigo's day violet and blue set apart.
- Rayleigh, the sky blue by Rayleigh scattering: the blue hour by night, a clear noon by day; the default flavor, by night and by day (`:colorscheme lumina` in Neovim follows it).
- Nebula, an emission nebula: oxygen cyan, sulphur gold, hydrogen rose on deep space; peacock ink on white by day.
- Harbor, harbour lights on navy water, a nautical chart by day; Dusk, a warm mid-dark evening in orchid and honey.
- At night the changed lines of diffs are violet, never brown; directories and their icons take one colour in every file browser and sidebar.
- One grammar for the writing modes (Org, Markdown, LaTeX, ConTeXt, HTML, CSS, YAML, BibTeX): eight heading levels, tags, keys and values in fixed roles; decorative faces drawn from the flavor's inks; one code for modal states across Emacs and Neovim.
- Targets: Emacs, Neovim, VS Code, WezTerm, base24.
- The family refounded: each flavor drawn from one scene and balanced on Rayleigh; new palettes for Cherenkov, Quartz, Dusk, Oxblood, Ember, Canopy, Harbor and Nebula.
- Aurora, green curtains over a violet polar night; Lagoon, coral on a mid-dark lagoon at dusk.
- One version per flavor: no background levels and no focus style; Emacs loads plain theme files, without lumina-themes-background and lumina-themes-style.
- Lapis, Indigo, Petrol, Mist and Rime archived in archive/; the former palettes of the kept flavors in archive/avant-famille/.
- Three warm flavors: Espresso, a café on a coffee-brown night; Olive, Provence on an olive night; Parchment, a reading room on tobacco and parchment.
- Ready for MELPA and the Marketplace: theme files provide their feature and pass package-lint; the VS Code extension shows as Lumina Themes.
- Screenshots taken in VS Code, Neovim and the terminal, on a demo project.
